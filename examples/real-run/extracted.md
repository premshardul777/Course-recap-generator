About Artificial Intelligence
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human
intelligence by computer systems. In 2026, it has moved
beyond simple automation to become an "agentic" force—
systems that can reason, remember, and act autonomously
to solve complex problems
About Technology
Technology is the application of scientific knowledge
for practical purposes, especially in industry. In 2026,
the boundary between "digital" and "physical" has
almost entirely dissolved, leading to a world where
technology is no longer just a tool we use, but an
environment we inhabit.

## Page 4

How AI "Works" in 2026
Multimodal Reasoning: Processing text, voice,
vision, and sensor data simultaneously.
Long-term Memory: Systems that remember
user preferences and past interactions across
different platforms.
Tool Use: AI agents now interact directly with
software APIs to book travel, write code, or
manage supply chains.

## Page 5

The Rise of Agentic Workflows
Definition: AI that doesn't just "chat" but
follows a multi-step plan to achieve a goal.
Example: A marketing agent that researches a
trend, creates content, schedules posts, and
adjusts the budget based on real-time
performance.
Impact: A fourfold increase in agentic AI
adoption in sectors like manufacturing.

## Page 6

AI in the Professional Workplace
The Digital Colleague: AI is no longer a "tool"
but a teammate.
Productivity Leap: Small teams (3–5 people)
can now execute global-scale projects by
delegating "data crunching" and "logistics" to
AI agents.
Key Skill: "Prompt Engineering" has evolved
into "Agent Orchestration"—the ability to
manage groups of AI agents.

## Page 7

Ethics, Bias, and Trust
The "Black Box" Problem: The challenge of
understanding why an AI made a specific decision.
Bias Mitigation: Active efforts to ensure AI doesn't
reinforce societal prejudices in hiring or lending.
Deepfakes & Identity: The shift in security from
protecting data to protecting "agency" (preventing
hijacked AI agents).

## Page 8

Regulation & The Law
EU AI Act: The world's first comprehensive framework is
now in full effect, setting the global standard.
Accountability: New laws require "Proof of Governance"—
companies must show their AI is auditable and safe.
Copyright: Ongoing evolution of laws regarding AI-
generated content and training data ownership.

## Page 9

Future Outlook: Toward AGI?
The Quest for AGI: Discussion on Artificial General
Intelligence (AI that equals human intelligence across all
tasks).
Energy Efficiency: The push for Sustainable AI as data center
power demands continue to rise.
Human-AI Sympathy: A focus on technology that amplifies
human creativity rather than replacing it.

## Page 10

Conclusion & Summary
Main Takeaway: 2026 is the year AI moved
beyond the screen and into the durable
foundations of our infrastructure.
Actionable Advice: Invest in governance, prioritize
human-AI collaboration, and stay adaptable.
